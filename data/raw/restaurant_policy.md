## Section 1: Physical Parameters & Operating Schedules
### 1.1 Location and Parking Infrastructure
Le Château Bistro is situated at its singular commercial address: 14 Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris, France. The premises do not contain a private, dedicated, or on-site customer parking lot. Guests utilizing private vehicles must be routed to the localized municipal infrastructure: public street parking is accessible at "Parking Vinci", which is located exactly 150 meters away from the primary restaurant entrance.

### 1.2 Standard Weekly Operational Hours
The restaurant maintains strict terminal door controls. Doors open promptly at 11:30 AM and close at 11:00 PM from Monday through Saturday. The facility is completely closed to the public on Sundays to execute essential weekly building maintenance and structural sanitation cycles.

### 1.3 Food and Beverage Kitchen Cutoff Framework
While the physical doors remain open until 11:00 PM, the back-of-house kitchen operations adhere to a strict separate timeline. The kitchen completely halts the ingestion of all new preparation orders at exactly 10:30 PM. Sitting down, placing terminal orders, or processing dine-in configurations at 10:45 PM is structurally impossible.

## Section 2: Conversational Boundary Controls & Escalation Triggers
### 2.1 Automated Reservation Caps
The platform's automated voice reservation processing script enforces a strict maximum safety capacity ceiling. Inbound callers may reserve table configurations automatically for party headcounts ranging between 1 and 8 guests. Any reservation request indicating a target party size parameter of 9 or more individuals breaks automated thresholds and must be classified as an Event / Large Gathering. The AI engine is forbidden from confirming these states and must immediately invoke the SIP telephony transfer bridge to route the audio stream directly to the events coordinator.

### 2.2 Inventory Boundaries & Domain Constraints
Le Château Bistro operates exclusively as a classic French restaurant vertical. The inventory database features items such as Charolais beef burgers, wild mushroom risottos, and curated regional wines. The restaurant does not prepare, stock, or offer sushi variations (such as spicy tuna rolls) or Italian fast-food elements (such as pepperoni pizzas). Any request seeking non-domain inventory must be flatly and politely denied.

### 2.3 Customer Conflict & Sentiment-Driven Escalation Protocols
If an inbound caller expresses immediate frustration, logs severe service complaints regarding historic orders (e.g., "my delivery order from last night arrived freezing cold"), or explicitly demands immediate transactional manipulation such as bank refunds, the conversational script must immediately stop automated sorting. The engine must express formal system empathy, log the baseline order details into the active session log, flag the case on the team's internal Slack dashboard, and execute an immediate, priority call transfer to the on-duty manager via the telephony bridge.
