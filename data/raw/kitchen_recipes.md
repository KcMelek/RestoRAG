Standard Component Yield per Plating Unit:

[CONDITIONAL PATHWAY FOR VEGAN PROFILES]
Operational Directive: The standard recipe incorporates traditional dairy-derived Grana Padano cheese, making the dish non-vegan by default. However, the line stations are stocked with an authorized plant-based substitute (Violife Provolone-Style Block). If the conversational agent registers an explicit user intent specifying a strict Vegan requirement, the assembly line is authorized to execute a complete ingredient swap, replacing the Grana Padano with the vegan cheese substitute to satisfy compliance.

[CRITICAL ENVIRONMENTAL ALLERGEN ALLOCATION]
Cross-Contamination Alert Matrix:
All line staff must remain cognizant that the designated preparation and pan-searing zone for the wild mushroom risotto directly shares physical counter space and storage trays with the pastry station's active pine nut prep line. Because trace airborne elements can settle across surfaces, the risotto cannot be guaranteed to be 100% free of nut variables. If a caller declares a highly sensitive, volatile, or life-threatening tree nut allergy, the conversational system must flag this shared tray environment explicitly to ensure complete transparency.
